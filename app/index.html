    Platinum Roadmap — Vice Got a Price!

    Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Figures: PowerPyx.

    1. Stage 1 — Finish the story (60–80 hours)

      Play the five chapters and finish every main mission. That earns all 12 story trophies, including the gold for the final mission, plus the stranger questlines as they unlock. Scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study new animals with binoculars as you go — both count toward the 100% checklist later. Do the Chapter 1 meditation (see the missable warning below) and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter: there is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a chapter-start save is your only safety net. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

    2. Stage 2 — The 100% completion checklist (about 100 hours)

      After the story the checklist unlocks under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, and everything on it can still be done in free-roam. Its thresholds are lenient — roughly 60% of true completion, per PowerPyx: 100 optional missions, 300 of 397 stunts, 30 unique races, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 Target Decks and all 42 shooting range golds. Finishing it earns the gold A Long Road, the hardest trophy on the list, and most other trophies along the way.

    3. Stage 3 — Mop-up (about 10 hours)

      Clean up what is left: stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks (8 of them), the last collectible card sets, the last WAiNK scans and meditation spots, and the miscellaneous one-off trophies (robberies, the tank, the plane at VCIA, the showroom Banshee). The platinum Vice Got a Price! pops when the 54th other trophy does.

    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

    PowerPyx and GTABase both flag it as the only trophy that may be missable. The first meditation is Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 — after the main mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends, and it is unconfirmed whether it is required, so do it and save before leaving the chapter. Everything else can be earned after the story.
